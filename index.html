    Family tree

    Intelligence test

    Every day a dozen creatures of each species run through the same small arenas as creatures with random brains. This is how much more food they find, or prey they catch.

    Language

    Every creature can make a call that its own kind hears up to 40 pixels away. Calls cost a little energy and start out meaningless. If a species starts calling much more when danger or food is near, a meaning is evolving.
